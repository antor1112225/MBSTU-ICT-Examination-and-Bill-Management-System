01

Problem Statement & Motivation

At MBSTU ICT Department, concluding semester final examinations across 4 academic years requires calculating faculty remuneration for internal and external teachers across 14 statutory categories.

Manual Paper Bottlenecks: Legacy handwritten bill slips led to arithmetic errors, omission of continuous assessments, moderation disputes, and zero audit transparency across academic sessions.
  • Moderation Disputes: Allocating ৳18,000 pool manually across 4 members.
  • Ceiling Violations: Miscalculating Class Test limits based on course credit.
  • No Central Ledger: Teachers lacked visibility into billed vs disbursed dues.
  • External Examiners: Missing courier expense vouchers & parent university data.
