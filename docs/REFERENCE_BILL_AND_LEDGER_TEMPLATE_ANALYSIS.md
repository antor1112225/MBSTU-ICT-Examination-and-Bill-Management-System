## 5. Discrepancies, Missing Data & Unresolved Field Clarifications

1. **Official Remuneration Rate Ambiguities:**
   * Script evaluation is universally observed at **৳120/- per script** across all verified bills.
   * Lab exams are observed at **৳100/- per student** (regular sessional) and **৳300/- per student** in specific specialized labs (e.g. `ICT-2102` on Dr. Nargis's bill has 57 × 300 = ৳17,100).
   * Tabulation is observed at **৳75/- per examinee**.
   * Chairman allowance is observed at **৳2,250/-**.
   * Question typing and verification is observed at **৳200/- per question**.
   * **Rule Implemented:** The system preserves configurable rate tables (`DEFAULT_MBSTU_RATES`) while dynamically populating verified rates from database records.
2. **External Teachers Exclusion:**
   * Physical bill slips REF-BILL-04, 05, 06 show external teachers submitting bills to MBSTU.
   * However, per strict user requirement, external candidates (specifically Rakib Rafiq Ull Kamal and aliases) are barred from internal department course allocations and internal faculty ledgers. Their historical data is audited separately in `research/EXCLUDED_TEACHER_AUDIT.md`.
3. **1Y 1S Laboratory Count Exemption:**
   * The 1Y 1S curriculum syllabus explicitly contains only **two (2) laboratory courses** (`ICT 1102` and `ICT 1104`). The system allocates both available labs and three theory courses, fulfilling the 5-course quota without fabricating an artificial 3rd lab.
